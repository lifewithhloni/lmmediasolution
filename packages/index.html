Recurring costs not included

The following third-party costs are not included in package prices and are paid by the client directly or quoted separately:

  • Domain registration and renewal
  • Website hosting
  • Business email subscriptions (for example Google Workspace or Microsoft 365)
  • Paid plugins, fonts, stock imagery or other third-party subscriptions

We can assist with domain, hosting and business email setup.
