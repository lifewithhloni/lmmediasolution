What makes a useful business website

Built to inform and make contact simple

Clear service information

Explain what you do, who you help and what customers can expect, in language they can scan on a phone.

Easy ways to enquire

Give visitors clear contact options, such as a form, phone number or WhatsApp link, where appropriate for your business.

Search-ready foundations

Use descriptive page titles, useful headings, sensible page structure and content that reflects your real services and service area.

Common questions

Website design FAQs

Do you design websites for businesses in Soweto?

Yes. LM Media Solutions works with businesses in Soweto and across Gauteng. Get in touch with a short project brief so we can discuss the right approach.

How much does a business website cost?

The current listed packages start at R3,999 for a 5-page website. Standard and Premium packages are listed at R6,999 and R9,999. Final requirements and scope should be confirmed before the project begins.

Can my website help customers contact me?

Website packages include contact features such as forms and WhatsApp integration as specified above. We’ll confirm which features suit your project.

Will my website automatically appear at the top of Google?

No website can promise a particular Google position. A useful, accessible site with clear service information gives search engines a better understanding of your business, while visibility develops through indexing, relevance and your business’s wider online presence.
