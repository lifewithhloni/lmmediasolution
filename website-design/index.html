Scope

What may require a separate quotation

The following are not included in the Bronze, Silver or Gold packages and are quoted separately:

  • E-commerce
  • Payment gateways
  • Custom dashboards
  • Authentication and complex databases
  • Booking systems
  • AI agents and advanced integrations
  • Paid third-party subscriptions
  • Ongoing maintenance outside the agreed support period

Domain, hosting and email

Domain registration, hosting and business email subscriptions are not included in website package prices. We can assist with setting them up on your accounts, and we explain the recurring costs before you commit.

  • Domain registration and renewal
  • Website hosting
  • Business email subscriptions (for example Google Workspace or Microsoft 365)
  • Paid plugins, fonts, stock imagery or other third-party subscriptions
