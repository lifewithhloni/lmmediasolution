Business Branding Packages: What Does Your Business Actually Need?

The right branding package depends on your stage of business and the opportunities you want to pursue. Here is how to decide.

By Lehlohonolo Thomas Maishoane · Published 22 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

Business branding packages are designed to bundle the assets a business commonly needs at a particular stage. Choosing the right package avoids paying for materials you will not use, while making sure nothing essential is missing. The best way to decide is to start with where your business is today and where you want it to go next.

Stage 1: Starting a new business

A new business needs a dependable foundation that allows it to present itself professionally from the first enquiry. Business startup branding typically includes:

  • A logo and basic brand identity, including colours and fonts
  • Business card design
  • A professional letterhead for quotes, invoices and letters
  • A branded email signature

These brand identity basics are enough to begin operating consistently. Our Business Starter package covers exactly these items, with an indicative range of R2,499–R3,499.

Stage 2: Improving your professional presentation

Once a business has customers and is looking to grow, it usually needs more than stationery. Prospective clients want to read about your services and check you online. A coordinated set of branding and website materials includes:

  • Brand identity and business stationery design
  • A company profile
  • A professional business website
  • A branded email signature

Our Professional Business package combines these, with an indicative range of R7,999–R12,999.

Stage 3: Pursuing corporate opportunities

Consultants, contractors and B2B suppliers approaching larger clients need materials that can stand up to procurement processes and boardroom meetings:

  • A complete brand identity system
  • A corporate company profile
  • A capability statement
  • A pitch deck or corporate presentation
  • A professional website
  • A full business stationery suite

Our Corporate Ready package is designed for this stage, with an indicative range of R14,999–R24,999+.

Do you need a package at all?

Not every business needs a bundle. If you already have a strong brand and only need a website, our standalone Bronze, Silver and Gold website packages are available separately. Likewise, individual services such as a company profile or presentation can be quoted on their own.

Questions to ask before choosing

  • What materials do customers and partners see most often?
  • Are you approaching new types of clients in the next twelve months?
  • Do you already have brand assets that can be kept or refreshed?
  • Will you supply the content, or do you need help shaping it?

Understanding package pricing

Package prices are indicative ranges, because the final scope depends on factors such as the complexity of the identity, the length of the company profile and the size of the website. At LM Media Solutions, scope and pricing are confirmed in writing before work begins, so you know exactly what you will receive. Recurring costs such as domain, hosting and email subscriptions are separate.
