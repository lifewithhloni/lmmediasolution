How Much Does Website Design Cost in South Africa?

Website design cost depends on scope, content, functionality and support. Here is what drives the price, and how our Bronze, Silver and Gold packages compare.

By Lehlohonolo Thomas Maishoane · Published 22 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

"How much does a website cost?" is one of the first questions business owners ask, and the honest answer is that it depends on what the website needs to do. Website design prices in South Africa vary widely, from do-it-yourself template builders to large agency projects. Understanding what drives the cost will help you compare quotes fairly and choose the right level for your business.

What affects website design cost

Number of pages

A one-page website that introduces your business and lists your services requires far less design and development time than a ten-page site with separate service, industry and blog pages.

Custom design versus templates

A pre-built template is cheaper to set up but can look similar to many other sites. Custom design aligned with your brand takes more time but gives your business a more distinctive and credible presence.

Content and copywriting

Someone has to write the words and source the images. If you supply finished text and photographs, the project is simpler. If the website designer also writes or edits the copy, the scope increases.

Functionality and integrations

Contact forms, WhatsApp buttons and basic SEO metadata are relatively standard. Online shops, payment gateways, booking systems, customer accounts and custom integrations require considerably more development and testing, and are usually quoted separately.

Revisions and support

The number of revision rounds and any post-launch support period affect the total project time. Clear limits protect both you and the designer from misunderstandings.

Recurring costs to budget for

The design and development fee is not the only cost of running a business website. Make sure you know whether these are included in a quote:

  • Domain registration and annual renewal
  • Website hosting
  • Business email subscriptions
  • Paid plugins, fonts, imagery or other third-party services
  • Ongoing maintenance and updates after launch

At LM Media Solutions, these recurring third-party costs are not included in our package prices. We can assist with domain, hosting and business email setup, and we explain the costs before you commit.

How to get an accurate quote

To receive a realistic quote from any provider, prepare the following:

  • A short description of your business and target customers
  • The pages you think you need
  • Whether you will supply the text and images
  • Any special functionality, such as online payments or bookings
  • Examples of websites you like, and why
  • Your budget range and preferred timeline

Choosing value over the lowest price

Affordable website design for a small business does not mean choosing the cheapest option. The right question is whether the website explains your offer clearly, works well on mobile devices and gives visitors a simple next step. A clearly scoped project avoids surprises for both sides.
